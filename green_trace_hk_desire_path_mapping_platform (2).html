Tactical Urbanism & Civic Engagement

Where Citizens Walk,
Desire Paths Reveal Real Needs.

Ever noticed informal dirt tracks carved into grass slopes or broken fences near MTR exits? These citizen shortcuts ("desire paths") highlight flawed urban design, safety risks, and soil erosion. Green Trace HK connects citizen mapping directly with HK Government departments to construct safe, paved walkways or protect green spaces.
